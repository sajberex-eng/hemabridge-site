Technologies and types of care

Each direction has its own route. It always starts with a doctor's assessment and ends with follow-up care by a hematologist at home.

Bone marrow transplantation (BMT)

A treatment using the patient's own or donor blood-forming stem cells. We help assess eligibility, arrange pre-transplant testing, prepare for treatment, and coordinate follow-up care.

  2. HLA and donor matching

CAR-T cell therapy

An advanced treatment that modifies a patient's immune cells to recognize cancer cells. It is used for selected types of leukemia, lymphoma, and multiple myeloma.

  1. Candidate evaluation
  2. Diagnostics
  3. Preparation
  4. Stage abroad

Modern Drug Therapies

Targeted medicines and immunotherapies act on specific disease mechanisms. Treatment selection depends on the diagnosis, previous therapies, and availability of medicines.

A consultation with an international specialist can help confirm a diagnosis and evaluate treatment options. In many cases, this can be arranged remotely without travelling abroad.
